How a web crawl works

A crawler walks a docs site by following links. Step through a small site and watch what it fetches, what it skips and why the page limit matters.

  1. Start from one address (start_url).
  2. Fetch the page and read its links.
  3. Queue links inside the section (include_urls). Skip the rest.
  4. Repeat until the queue is empty or the limit (max_pages) is reached.
